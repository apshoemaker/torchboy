> * let's work on making the levels procedurally generated.  They should be roughly the same size that they are now, but they should be different each run.

## Procedural caves

Generated in the browser at load, different every run, in ~10–19ms. This meant porting the cavern mesh out of Blender into `CavernMesh.js` — ~65k triangles built at runtime, which also **deletes the 2.4MB `cavern.glb`**. `public/` is now 500KB, all of it the character and props.

Levels that fail their own invariants are **re-rolled rather than shipped**: the way onward must be reachable without any secret, at least one reward must be genuinely gated behind one, and the cave must land in the size band the hand-tuned ones occupied. Across 12 runs: 435–609 floor tiles, minimum 2 secrets — a bad CA roll was otherwise handing out 260-tile caves that are over in a minute.

## He climbs

Levels stack upward now, he wakes in The Deep Hollow, and the way onward is a staircase he walks up rather than a shaft he steps around.

## The ending

Control is taken away — this is the one moment to be watched, not played. He walks the last steps, the arch light swells until it's all that's left, and he comes apart upward: 420 motes leaving him while his body thins, the screen going to daylight *while* he's still dissolving so it reads as one event. The motes rise rather than scatter; falling would read as destruction.

Then Claude writes the close, knowing the whole run:

> *"He walked into the light because a promise made too young does not care how old you get to keep it."*

## The story, written as you play

`Chronicle.js` logs what he actually does — how far he's walked with nothing to show for it, that his torch went cold at a wall and he walked away anyway, that he's begun retreading his own footprints. Every request sends that plus everything already told, so it continues rather than restarts. Beats are **prefetched**, so the ~3s write never shows.

One run produced this, entirely from play:

> He woke with the blue string still knotted round his wrist…
> **The honour given to boys with string is the same honour given to what is buried.**
> *He touches the string and understands, resigned, that he was sent as an offering, not a hope.*
> …He knows now the climb was never his; his legs only walk where the string still leads.

~$0.03–0.05 per playthrough.

## Two bugs worth flagging

**A premise race.** The first beat and first revelation fired in parallel before a premise existed, so the server minted two — the story was about a glove while the revelations were about a key. The first request now goes alone.

**The ending kept getting wiped.** Once the ascension finished, the normal loop resumed and its own `hud.fade(0)` erased the whiteout.

I'll also own a process failure: one of my patches silently no-opped on a stale anchor and I reported success without checking, so `narrator.finish` didn't exist and the ending card came up blank. I've since been asserting the change landed before printing success — which caught a second bad anchor later in the same session.
